    How to play Mattis

    Card order, lowest to highest: 2–10, J, Q, K, A. Play goes clockwise.

    Round 1: Collecting

    • Everyone always holds 3 cards. When you lay one, you draw a new one.
    • Instead of laying from your hand, you can press Chance card on the deck: the top card of the deck is laid instead, and your hand stays the same.
    • Everyone lays one card. When all have laid, the highest card takes the whole stack.
    • But if two or more cards have the same value (two 3s, two kings…), those players battle: each lays another card, and the highest takes it all, including any higher card on the table. Tie again? Battle again.
    • More than one pair (say two 6s and two 9s)? Only the highest pair battles.
    • Don't want the stack? Lay low and let someone else take it.
    • Whoever takes the stack starts the next round.
    • The last card in the deck is the hidden trump. Whoever would draw it gets it, revealed when the deck is gone.

    Round 2: Get rid of your cards

    • Your collected cards (plus any left in your hand) become your hand. The trump holder starts.
    • Beat the top card with a higher card of the same suit, or with a trump. Over a trump you need a higher trump.
    • Got cards of the same suit in a row, like 5♥ 6♥ 7♥ 8♥? Lay them all at once as a run. The lowest card must beat the top card, and the run counts as one lay.
    • Can't or won't beat it? Pick up the first card laid on the stack.
    • When every player has laid a card, the stack goes out and whoever played last starts again.
    • Run out of cards and you're safe. The last player holding cards loses.
