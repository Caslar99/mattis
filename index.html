How to play Mattis

Card order, lowest to highest: 2–10, J, Q, K, A. Play goes clockwise.

Round 1: Collecting

  • Everyone always holds 3 cards. When you play one, you draw a new one.
  • Instead of playing from your hand, you can gamble: flip the top card of the deck and play that.
  • A battle is between two players. One lays a card and the next answers. A higher answer takes the whole stack, and a lower answer gives it to the first player.
  • Same rank? They battle on until one of them takes it all.
  • Don't want the stack? Play lower and lose on purpose.
  • Whoever takes the stack starts the next battle.
  • The last card in the deck is the hidden trump. Whoever would draw it gets it, revealed when the deck is gone.

Round 2: Get rid of your cards

  • Your collected cards (plus any left in your hand) become your hand. The trump holder starts.
  • Beat the top card with a higher card of the same suit, or with a trump. Over a trump you need a higher trump.
  • Can't or won't beat it? Pick up the first card laid on the stack.
  • When every player has laid a card, the stack goes out and whoever played last starts again.
  • Run out of cards and you're safe. The last player holding cards loses.
